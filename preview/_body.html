The science

Your nose is the bottleneck

Most of your airway resistance sits in the narrowest part of the nasal valve. Nurva's dual spring bands lift that valve open from the outside — no drugs, no sprays, nothing habit forming. Just a bigger pipe for the air you already breathe.

  • Dual-band spring tension

    Two flex bands instead of one, so the lift holds through sweat, movement and side sleeping.

  • Hypoallergenic adhesive

    Latex-free, skin-safe and gentle on removal — even on sensitive skin.

  • Contour-cut for the nasal valve

    Shaped to sit exactly where resistance is highest, not just across the bridge.

How Nurva works

No batteries, no refills, no learning curve. Peel, place, breathe.

1

Clean & dry

Wash and dry the bridge of your nose so the adhesive grips properly. No oils, no lotion.

2

Peel & place

Remove the liner and centre the strip just above the flare of your nostrils.

3

Press & hold

Press the ends down for five seconds. The spring bands do the rest.

4

Breathe

Train, race or sleep. Peel off gently under warm water when you're done.
